Temhiro (ተምህሮ / Tinat) is your all-in-one offline educational companion designed specifically for Ethiopian high school and university students. Whether preparing for the national Ethiopian University Entrance Examination (EUEE), freshman university modules, exit exams, or high school class tests, Temhiro equips you with verified curriculum resources right at your fingertips.

KEY FEATURES:

• 100% Offline Learning: Download once and revise anywhere without needing internet. All notes, textbooks, and flashcards remain accessible 24/7.
• Comprehensive EUEE & Entrance Exam Prep: Practice with past national examination questions, timed mock exams, and instant explanations.
• Interactive 3D Flashcards: Boost memory retention with spaced repetition flashcards covering Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Economics, and History.
• University & Freshman Modules: Structured notes covering introductory university courses and academic department curriculums.
• Multilingual Support: Study seamlessly in English, Amharic (አማርኛ), and Afaan Oromoo.
• Track Progress & Achievements: Monitor your unit completion, exam scores, and study streaks with dynamic mastery analytics.
