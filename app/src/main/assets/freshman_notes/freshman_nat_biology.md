A. Primary production
â– The flow of energy through a terrestrial ecosystem starts with the harnessing
of sunlight by green plants, a process that in itself demands the expenditure

â– A plant gets its start by living on the food energy stored in the seed until it

â– Energy accumulated by plants is called primary production, because it is the

â– Total energy assimilated by the plant is gross primary production.
â– Plants, like all other organisms, must expend energy in production,

â– This energy is produced by the process of respiration which liberates energy.
